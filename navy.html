What do I need to take the exam?

You need to be 18, have a Social Security number, and hold the 40-hour certificate from a licensed school like ours.

What if I miss a day?

Make up the matching day in a later class. Rebooking costs $25 for 1–2 days or $100 for 3 or more. Eden will set it up.

Do I really keep my camera on?

Yes. The state needs proof you attended all 40 hours, so stay visible with your full name under your video.

How fast can I get licensed?

Your certificate comes within 24 hours of class. Then you apply on eLIPSE; once approved, you book your PSI exam. We help with the application in class.

Can I cancel?

Email Eden before you enroll for the current cancellation and transfer policy.
